# Travel and expenses

Submit receipts in Expensify within 30 days of purchase. Expenses over 1,000 USD need your manager's approval before you spend the money.

Meals while travelling for work are covered up to 50 USD per day. Flights must be booked through the travel desk at travel@company.com: economy class for flights under 6 hours, premium economy for longer flights. Taxis are fine when public transport would take more than 45 minutes longer.
