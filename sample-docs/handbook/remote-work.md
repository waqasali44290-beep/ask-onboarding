# Remote and hybrid work

Most roles are hybrid: you work from the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays and from anywhere in your country on other days. Fully remote roles are agreed in your contract.

Every employee gets a one-time home-office budget of 500 USD for a desk, chair or monitor. Claim it through Expensify within your first 90 days. Working from another country for more than 2 weeks needs approval from HR because of tax rules.
