# Salary bands 2026 (managers only)

Salary bands for engineering roles, in USD per year:

- Junior engineer: 55,000 to 70,000
- Engineer: 70,000 to 95,000
- Senior engineer: 95,000 to 120,000
- Staff engineer: 120,000 to 150,000

Offers above the top of a band need approval from the VP of Engineering. Do not share these numbers with employees or candidates.
